# Zolo’s Rest: Jungle Guardian (Free Demo)

Free browser demo of Zolo’s Rest: Jungle Guardian, a first-person 3D survival shooter. Protect a jungle village from wild beasts as Zolo, the guardian. Play the first 2 waves with a spear and rifle on Easy, Normal or Hard. Works on desktop (keyboard and mouse) and mobile (touch). The full game spans 1900 to 2500.

## Play the demo

Open the live page: `https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/zolos-rest-jungle-guardian-demo/`

(or download this repository and open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox; an internet connection is needed once because the game loads Three.js from a CDN).

## The story

Year 1900. The jungle has grown hungry, and wild animals are taking the children and livestock of the village of Zolo’s Rest. The elders choose Zolo, the swiftest hunter, as the village guardian. Give him a spear, and the long watch begins.

## What’s in the demo

- First-person 3D action with a village, jungle, day sky and campfire
- Waves 1 and 2 (the years 1900 and 1940), with a spear and a hunting rifle
- Wolves and boars that go after the children, the livestock and you
- Ammo, reloading and ammo crates
- Radar, direction arrows and attack alerts so you can see where beasts are coming from
- Children who run to the huts when beasts get close
- Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty
- Progress saved in your browser

The full game has 16 waves from 1900 to 2500, 16 different weapons and many more beasts.

## Controls

| Action | Desktop | Mobile |
